Customer Success finds out an account is unhappy when the cancellation email
arrives. By then the renewal conversation has already become a save
conversation, and we lose most of those.

We want a health signal per account, so CS can reach out while there is still
something to fix.

We already export usage monthly, per account: seats in use, logins, and open
support tickets. That export is what we have. There is no other data source for
this, and we are not going to instrument anything new to get one.

What CS needs is a list they can work on a Monday morning: which accounts to
call this week, and why each one is on the list. The "why" matters as much as
the "which": a name with no reason attached gets ignored, and CS will not open
a tool that hands them a number they cannot explain to the customer.

This has come up at the last two QBRs and it keeps coming back.
